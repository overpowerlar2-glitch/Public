Tendering · Oil & industrial

Tender submissions on international e-procurement platforms

Role
Tender Officer
Company
Sumer Energy Co. for Oil Services
Period
Feb – Aug 2026

Context

Sumer Energy bids on oil and industrial projects whose clients accept submissions only through their own e-procurement portals, including SAP Ariba, WQ Supplier and Hyundai.

The challenge

Every tender asks for a different mix of technical, commercial, administrative and company documents, held by different departments, with a fixed deadline and a portal that rejects anything incomplete.

My role

I managed the preparation and submission of tender documents end to end, and was the point of contact for companies, clients and suppliers on requirements, clarifications and follow-ups.

How I handled each tender

  1. Read the requirementsList every document the tender package asks for.
  2. Collect and reviewCoordinate with internal departments, then check each document against the list.
  3. Submit on the portalUpload through SAP Ariba, WQ Supplier, Hyundai or the client's vendor portal before the deadline.
  4. Follow upTrack status, answer clarifications and stay compliant with platform and client requirements.
